The company

Technology, made for people.

Artist Point LLC builds software that is human-centered. We take our name from Artist Point, the high overlook on Kulshan.

Our work starts with the people who will use it. Then we build technology that serves them.

What we do

Crafted software for real work.

We design and build software: products, tools, and systems meant to last. Independent and rooted in the Pacific Northwest.

Headquarters

Between the sea and the Cascades.

We are based in Whatcom County, in the northwest corner of Washington.

Artist Point itself sits above 5,000 feet at the end of State Route 542. We borrowed the name.
